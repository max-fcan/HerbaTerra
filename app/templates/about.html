About HerbaTerra

A geography-first way to explore plant biodiversity.

{{ section_heading('The project') }}

HerbaTerra was built for the Trophees NSI 2026, a French national competition that highlights student work in computer science.

The app combines an interactive country hub, a filterable plant catalogue, and a location game inspired by GeoGuessr to make plant distribution easier to explore visually.

For CS50, this submission is presented as an individual project and is based on my own production work after a full refactor of the codebase.

Fun fact: 5% of world runs can land in Antarctica.
